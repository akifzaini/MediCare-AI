# HbA1c and Diabetes Screening (ADA / WHO)

HbA1c reflects average blood glucose over ~2–3 months. Standard categories:

- Normal: below 5.7% (below 39 mmol/mol)
- Prediabetes: 5.7%–6.4% (39–46 mmol/mol)
- Diabetes: 6.5% or above (48 mmol/mol or above), confirmed on repeat testing

An HbA1c of 5.4% is in the normal range with no sign of diabetes.

Source: American Diabetes Association (Standards of Care); WHO guidance on HbA1c.
Educational reference only; not a diagnosis.
