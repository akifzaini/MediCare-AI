# Adult Health Screening Overview (MOH Malaysia)

Routine screening helps detect common conditions early. General adult guidance:

- Blood pressure
- Blood glucose / HbA1c (diabetes risk)
- Lipid profile (total, LDL, HDL, triglycerides)
- BMI and waist circumference
- Kidney function (creatinine / eGFR) where indicated

Frequency depends on age, family history and risk. Results should be discussed with a
qualified healthcare professional; screening is not a self-diagnosis tool.

Source: Ministry of Health Malaysia (MOH). Educational reference only; not a diagnosis.
