# eGFR and Kidney Function (KDIGO / National Kidney Foundation)

eGFR estimates how well the kidneys filter waste, in mL/min/1.73m². Stages:

- 90 or above: normal or high (G1, if no other markers of kidney damage)
- 60–89: mildly decreased (G2)
- 45–59 (G3a) / 30–44 (G3b): moderate decrease
- 15–29: severe decrease (G4); below 15: kidney failure (G5)

An eGFR of 96 indicates healthy filtration. Creatinine rises as filtration slows.

Source: KDIGO CKD Guideline; U.S. National Kidney Foundation. Educational reference only; not a diagnosis.
