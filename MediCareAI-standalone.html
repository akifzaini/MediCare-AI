Executive Summary

Medical Simplifier · plain-English explanation

Overall, your blood test looks mostly healthy. Your blood sugar control and kidney function are well within normal ranges. Two areas deserve attention: your hemoglobin is slightly low, which can mean your body has less iron than it would like — this often explains tiredness — and your LDL ("bad") cholesterol is mildly elevated, something usually improved with diet and exercise. Neither finding is an emergency, but both are worth discussing with your doctor at your next visit.
